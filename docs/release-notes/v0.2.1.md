ZeroIn 0.2.1 adds a real polling-rate check with device identification, the polling-jitter validity rule for sensitivity tests, and optional mousepad and mouse-feet notes.

**Downloads**
- `ZeroIn-Setup-0.2.1-x64.exe`: installer, includes the local AI model (Qwen3 1.7B, Q4_K_M, about 1.1 GB).
- `ZeroIn-0.2.1-x64-portable.zip`: app only, no model. Works fully without the AI.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: checksums for both files.

**Added**
- **Check mouse** (Settings → Devices). Move the mouse fast for five seconds. ZeroIn reads Raw Input in buffered batches on a high-priority thread and reports the effective rate, median-interval rate, 1st/99th-percentile intervals, jitter (std and MAD), skipped polls, bunched delivery, stability over time, a rate graph and an interval histogram.
- **Verdict** (Good / OK / Problem) against the rate set in your mouse software, with the likely source: mouse, USB port or wireless link, PC, or movement. Flags a 4000–8000 Hz setting that is not delivered when neither slow movement nor the PC explains it.
- **Device identity** from the device itself: product and manufacturer strings and USB/Bluetooth vendor and product ID. Wireless receivers are shown as receivers. Optional link to EloShapes in your browser; no data is fetched from it.
- **Polling-jitter rule**: test blocks with chaotic report timing run again instead of being scored.
- **Optional gear**: mousepad (surface, speed class, name) and mouse feet in Setup, stored with results, shown in Result and History, with a History filter and comparison by mousepad. Gear never changes the measured sensitivity or scores; when filled in, the result shows a short, cited note based on public data.
- **Gear lookup** (opt-in, off by default): opens a browser search for the typed mousepad name. The app makes no network connection itself.

**Limits**
- Check mouse timestamps are arrival times at ZeroIn; device-side timing is not visible from Windows. Verified with synthetic streams, not yet on real high-rate hardware.
