ZeroIn 0.2.0 replaces the search with one that keeps your current sensitivity unless another clearly scores better, and shows a range of about-equally-good settings instead of a single number with a confidence percentage.

**Downloads**
- `ZeroIn-Setup-0.2.0-x64.exe`: installer, includes the local AI model (Qwen3 1.7B, Q4_K_M, about 1.1 GB).
- `ZeroIn-0.2.0-x64-portable.zip`: app only, no model. Works fully without the AI.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: checksums for both files.

**Changed**
- A test is a budget of short blocks: Quick 6 (about 2.5 min) or Standard 9 (about 4 min). Three bracket blocks (×1, ×1.6, ×0.625) are followed by adaptive blocks placed from a Bayesian model of your scores, started from a per-game prior built from public pro-settings statistics.
- A change is recommended only when the predicted gain reaches the mode's threshold: Conservative 2 points, Balanced 1 (default), Explorer 0.5. Otherwise the result is "Keep your current sens".
- Each block: 2 warm-up flicks (not scored), 6 flicks, 5 micro-adjusts, 5 s of tracking. Same targets every block, new order; sizes by game type. Tracking is measured over raw-input time, so frame rate does not affect it.
- The local AI only writes the explanation and cannot change any number.

**Added**
- Progress bar and time left; resume an interrupted test from Setup within 24 hours.
- Blocks hit by stutter, dropped input, anticipated clicks or repeated timeouts run again (up to twice); fewer than 6 usable blocks gives "Test incomplete".
- Point or comma decimals; DPI 1.600 or 1,600 is read as 1600. Pixel-skip warning with a same-cm/360 fix.

**Fixed**
- Aim-bias advice is corrected for the typical 11 % undershoot and no longer pushes results faster.
- The drill crosshair is sharp at 150 % display scaling.

0.1 history still opens. See CHANGELOG.md for details and known limitations.
